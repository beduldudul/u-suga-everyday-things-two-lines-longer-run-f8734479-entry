Line 1 explains a traffic jam to a 10-year-old through one analogy — toothpaste stuck in the tube. Line 2 says why the analogy works: what it maps (the squeeze, the slow ooze) and what it leaves out (toothpaste has no choice; drivers could take another road).

## Entry

> A traffic jam is toothpaste stuck in the tube: too many cars trying to squeeze out of a tiny opening at once.
> It maps the squeeze and the slow ooze, but it leaves out that toothpaste has no choice and cars do — drivers could take another road.
